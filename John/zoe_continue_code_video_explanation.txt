I added a health bar to the player and enemies, and gave them the ability to deal damage to each other. The enemy deals damage by coming into contact with the player. And though the video doesn't capture this aspect very well as I did not add an attack animation, the player can attack the enemy by left-clicking when next to it. In the video it can be seen how the enemy scores a hit on the player and the player then attacks the enemy back until it dies. The combat system is still somewhat buggy at the moment.
